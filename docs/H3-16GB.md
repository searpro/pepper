# MiniMax H3 on a 16 GB card

How to get publishable video out of MiniMax H3 on the home box (RTX 5060 Ti,
16 GB VRAM, 32 GB RAM, 1 TB disk, no hourly bill), from the community's
measured findings (October 2026) and our own runs. Section 5 is what Pepper
Pro does with it; section 6 holds our measurements.

## 1. In short

| Lever | What works on 16 GB | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Diffusion model | Pruned FL2VA in **NVFP4** (11.7 GB, Abiray/coolthor), on the CUDA 13 image | Fits the card whole; Blackwell runs FP4 natively. Fallbacks: official pruned `w6a8` (16.0 GB), pruned int8 ConvRot (21 GB, spills to RAM) |
| Text encoder | Official `qwen3vl_32b_minimax_h3_nvfp4_awq` (14.6 GB) | Runs once per prompt and steps aside. GGUF builds are *larger* (Q4_K_M 19.8 GB) and need a forked loader; 4B/8B ClipProj swaps cost quality |
| Attention | Comfy Kitchen INT8 attention | 6.3× faster than SDPA on H3's geometry at 1.6% error; the sampler halves. Needs cu130 |
| Steps | 20 steps (res_multistep, simple, shift 12/3) for every take; turbo only to block a shot | At 960×544, 20 steps take about 290 s per 6 s, less than an 8-step turbo draft at 1344×768 (390 s). Turbo loses facial detail and voices (Continuum, FlowForgeLabAi, our Mei take) |
| Turbo on the pruned base | A LoRA converted for pruned bases | A full-base LoRA applies 208 of 259 modules on a pruned base: the 51 AdaLN layers (the timestep path a distillation changes) are skipped |
| Size | Takes at about 0.5 MP (960×544), mastered to 1920×1088 by H3 itself | The field's consensus (obvpm, zuanfilm, AIMixer, RunningHub dual-sampling): sample the motion small, then let H3 redraw the texture at 2 MP from its own latents. Faces want close and medium shots; wide-shot faces need Face Refine |
| Length | 5–15 s per render (124–362 frames on the 17k+5 grid) | Longer is untested; on a 24 GB 4090, steps stall past 345 frames |
| RAM | The real wall | A 34 GiB working set pages on 16 GB RAM; the tell is flat ~35 W GPU power at 99% "utilisation" |
| Prompts | MiniMax's structured format | The single biggest quality lever in the field reports (§3) |
| Long scenes | Shots continuing each other **from saved latents** (39-frame pins), each re-anchored on the character's reference pictures, mastered as one piece | Pixel continuation (decode → encode the tail) is where our Mei cut fell apart; every chained pack still drifts in contrast and skin over many hops (§4), so keep a run short and cut |
| Delivery | Master only the chosen takes: H3 latent upscale + an H3 re-sample at denoise 0.2–0.3; RTX VSR for 4K | SeedVR2 on a decoded H3 take sharpens the compression and the wobble with it; H3's own re-sample draws coherent skin. Save every intermediate at CRF ≤ 14: ComfyUI's automatic encode stored our takes at ~2 Mbps |

## 2. Models and files

The Comfy-Org repo is the reference set; ComfyUI's own templates use the
pruned int8 ConvRot model, the NVFP4 text encoder, and the video and audio
VAEs (about 40 GB). On a 16 GB card the parts take turns: the encoder
encodes and steps aside, the diffusion model samples, the VAEs decode.

- **Diffusion model, smallest first:** pruned NVFP4 11.7 GB (needs CUDA 13
  torch: under cu128, comfy_kitchen has no FP4 matmul and its eager
  fallback ran our SCAIL-2 NVFP4 test out of memory), W4A8 ConvRot 11.7 GB,
  pruned w6a8 16.0 GB (official), pruned int8 ConvRot / fp8 19.5 GB.
  GGUF: unsloth's pruned Q4_K 10.6 GB, Q5_0 13.0 GB, Q6_K 15.5 GB (needs
  ComfyUI-GGUF and an architecture patch). FL2VA does text, first-frame,
  first-and-last and last-frame video; Ref2VA does up to 9 images, 3 clips
  and 3 voice clips as references.
- **Turbo LoRAs:** the official lightx2v 8-step (`fl2v_turbo_8step_v1.0_comfyui`)
  is the shared base. For pruned checkpoints use pruned conversions:
  drbaph's `…_pruned_comfyui`, FastVideo's FastH3 4-step (drozbay's pruned
  r128 extraction, "recommended"), HardGravy's 6-step merge (lightx2v +
  larryvrh + a photorealism LoRA, author-tested on a 16 GB A4000 with the
  pruned int8: ~330 s per 10 s at 0.5 MP, better audio and adherence).
  Wuli-Art's 8-step "unlocked" models take any scheduler at 6–12 steps.
- **VAE:** the int8 ConvRot video VAE (2.6 GB) halves the fp16 one; the audio
  VAE stays fp32 (lower precision desyncs audio and video). The decode is a
  memory burst at the end: a run that dies after its last step died there.
- **Latent upscaler** (LBH-123-AI, 691 MB): a neural 2× on H3's latents for a
  second pass, skipping the VAE round trip; it saves time, not VRAM.

## 3. Prompts

MiniMax publishes the formats H3 was trained on
(`MiniMaxAI/MiniMax-H3/docs/VIDEO_PROMPT_WRITING_GUIDE_{base,ref}_en.md`):

- **Base (FL2VA):** a frame-alignment line first (for a first frame, a last
  frame, or both, with the last aligned to the clip's length), then three
  fields: `integrated_multimodal_description` (`[Shot 1] style, composition…
  [Shot 2] At 00:04.000, the camera cuts to …`), `overall_soundscape`
  (ambience and physical sounds) and `non_diegetic_music` (or `N/A`).
- **Reference (Ref2VA):** six sections: `subject_definitions` binding each
  `<Subject n>` to its `<Picture n>` (and `<Audio n>` voices), `summary` with
  a task tag, `retention_analysis`, `detailed_description`, and the two
  sound fields.
- **Speakers** get stable `(S1)` IDs with a voice description; their words go
  verbatim into `<d>[English] …</d>`.
- **Camera** is type + amplitude + speed + where it ends ("pushes in with
  small amplitude at slow speed toward her hands"). A move with no
  destination overshoots; a 10 s clip whose prompt covers only its opening
  loses its subject at ~7 s (FlowForgeLabAi reproduced both on the official
  20-step baseline).

## 4. Long, consistent video

The community's "director" packs (H3-Multishot, MiniMax H3 Director,
LongMedia Director, H3 Extender, Motion Context) share one mechanism: each
shot is its own prompt, rendered continuing the last frames of the one
before (22 frames pinned at the start, trimmed at the join: 124 + 124 − 22
= 226). On top:

- **Identity:** reference images per subject (Ref2VA), or starting each shot
  from a real rendered frame of the character; feeding generated frames back
  as references compounds invented detail.
- **Seams:** H3-Multishot matches each shot's luma and contrast to the first
  and welds audio in the quietest gap; matsuo-koya measured colour bias
  under 1/255 and dropped seam correction. Drift is about 1.05 per hop and
  fine texture grows about 13% per join over 7 windows: keep a take to
  about four.
- **Retakes:** re-render one window and keep the rest.
- A reported pipeline: a continuous 40 s Full HD video in about 42 min on a
  16 GB card (two H3 segments with motion context, a split sampler that
  starts at low resolution, then a tiled "Ultimate Upscale" pass).

### What degrades a long take, and what helps

Our first Mei cut (two 6 s shots, the second continuing the first) was clean
in shot 1 and fell apart in shot 2: hair to a black mass, the raincoat
mottled, the bus glass smeared, the face texture shimmering. Three causes,
each fixed in Pepper Pro now:

1. **Pixel continuation.** Shot 2 was guided by shot 1's last 22 frames
   *decoded to an MP4 and encoded again*. A VAE round trip is lossy, and the
   new shot starts from a softened copy. obvpm keeps every take's raw
   latents beside it (`.mctx`) and pins them directly (latent masks, native
   H3 keyframe anchors), so the join is exact.
2. **A turbo LoRA that does not fit the pruned model** (208 of 259 patches,
   §6), at 8 steps. Twenty full steps at 0.5 MP cost less than that draft did
   at 1 MP.
3. **Compression between stages.** ComfyUI's automatic encode stored the takes
   at 1.8–2.3 Mbps, and SeedVR2 then upscaled the H.264 artefacts.

What the field reports about drift across many hops (October 2026):

- **It accumulates in every chaining pack.** H3 Continuum's issue #13: each
  chunk comes out more contrasty, skin "oilier"; by six chunks (60 s) the
  faces are barely legible. Its author traced the worst of it to feeding the
  previous chunk back as a *reference* (fixed in v3.6, "Standard" continuation
  with 22-frame masked AV context) and found that reusing a **fixed prefix**
  reduced the rest; ComfyUI-Continuity's issue #41 reports the same softening
  and contrast creep. Nobody claims a complete fix.
- **Re-anchor on clean sources, not on generated frames.** Herrgotts' suite
  gives each segment "a quality reset and fresh keyframe anchors";
  Ref2VA shots keep the character's own pictures as `<Picture n>` every
  shot. A continuing `h3-reference` shot in Pepper Pro does exactly this:
  latent pins for motion and sound, the asset's pictures for identity.
- **Restore the seam before handing it on.** Continuity's *Seam Restore*
  re-samples the tail that the next shot will continue at denoise 0.45, so
  softness does not compound. obvpm's equivalent is bridging onto a fresh
  generation, which "brings the quality back if it has drifted".
- **Refine the whole run together.** A refine re-invents most fine texture
  (obvpm measured 74% of it moving away from the upscaled prior), so two
  takes refined apart disagree at the join however exact the hold. obvpm's
  joint refine samples the run as one latent in overlapping windows; this is
  Pepper Pro's master (§5). RunningHub's Motion Context V2 avoids two-pass
  chains for the same reason ("a pixel shift at every join").
- **Cut.** Films cut; a scene of shots that each start fresh from the
  references and a clean keyframe, joined by real cuts, never accumulates
  drift. Keep continuing runs to a few shots (obvpm, matsuo-koya: about four
  windows) and cut between them.

**NVIDIA's VFX nodes** ([Comfy-Org/ComfyUI_Nvidia_VFX_Nodes](https://github.com/Comfy-Org/ComfyUI_Nvidia_VFX_Nodes),
[blog](https://blog.comfy.org/p/nvidia-vfx-nodes-for-video-upscaling)): RTX Video Super Resolution (up to 4×
or 8K, LOW…ULTRA and HIGHBITRATE modes, denoise and deblur), RTX frame generation (2–16×, 40-series and
later) and SDR→HDR. They need CUDA 13 torch (our cu130 image) and `nvidia-vfx` (Linux wheels from
pypi.nvidia.com). The upscale runs one frame at a time and adds nothing of its own: it cannot make a face
shimmer, and cannot repair one that does. It is the right last step from a 1080p master to 1440p or 4K, not
a substitute for the H3 re-sample. The node holds the whole result in RAM as float32 (about 100 MB per 4K
frame).

## 5. What Pepper Pro does

- **`16gb` tier on the CUDA 13 image** (`pepper-pro:cu130`, picked by the
  home launcher when the driver supports CUDA 13), with NVFP4 variants where
  a recipe has them.
- **Comfy Kitchen attention for every recipe**, switched on at ComfyUI start
  when the image has it (`COMFY_CK_ATTENTION`).
- **Shot prompts composed in H3's formats** (`prompt_format: h3-base |
  h3-reference`): alignment line, the three or six fields, speaker IDs,
  `<d>` dialogue, `<Subject n>` / `<Picture n>` / `<Audio n>` bindings; a
  shot's `sound` splits at "Music:". A prompt already in the format is used
  as written. The planner and plan_project ask for whole-shot action,
  destination cameras and timed beats.
- **Takes are 960×544 at 20 steps** (`final`, the default mode; `draft` is
  the 8-step turbo, for blocking a shot), saved by obvpm's
  `H3TrimAndSaveVideoWithMCtx` at CRF 12 with their latents (`.mctx`) and
  conditioning (`.cond`) beside them. The sidecars travel with the take into
  the project.
- **Continuing shots continue from the latents.** A shot marked `continues`
  is rendered from the previous shot's chosen take through obvpm's
  `H3LoadMCtx` (39-frame pin) and `H3MCtxApplyPins`; the new take is saved
  with the pinned frames trimmed off, so the cut trims nothing and the join
  is exact. `h3-reference` continues too, keeping its reference pictures, so
  identity is re-anchored every shot. A take without latents (older takes)
  still continues through the decoded tail (`previous`).
- **Long takes** chain segments through H3's continuation, as before.
- **Mastering is H3 re-sampling its own latents** (`h3-master`): the take's
  run of continuing shots is joined into one latent, upscaled 2× by the H3
  latent upscaler with temporal chunking, re-sampled by H3 (10 steps,
  denoise 0.2) in 5 s windows overlapping by 1.25 s, each under its own
  take's conditioning, with the sound held, and decoded with the fp16 VAE
  into one 1920×1088 file. The service splits it back into one master per
  take at their frames, and the cut plays those. Other takes are mastered
  by SeedVR2, as before. `rtx-upscale-video` takes a master on to 4K.
- **Every video recipe saves at CRF 14** (H3 takes at 12) instead of
  ComfyUI's automatic encode.
- **Cuts** export 1080 on the short side at −14 LUFS for every aspect.

## 6. Measured on the RTX 5060 Ti

Home box: RTX 5060 Ti 16 GB, Ryzen 5 7600X, 32 GB DDR5 (one stick, single
channel), driver 595, `pepper-pro` built for CUDA 13 (torch 2.14.1+cu130),
Comfy Kitchen attention on.

| Job | Files | Size / length | Time | Peak VRAM / RAM |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| SCAIL-2 draft (6 steps) | NVFP4 mix 11 GB | 640×640, 81 frames | 335–397 s (361 s warm) | 12.1 GB / 19 GB |
| SCAIL-2 draft | int8 ConvRot 16.7 GB | 640×640, 81 frames | 385 s | — / 26 GB |
| SCAIL-2 NVFP4 on the **CUDA 12.8** image | NVFP4 mix | — | out of memory after 33 s | 14.1 GiB allocated |
| SeedVR2 master, 2× | 3B int8 | 640² → 1280², 81 frames | 247 s | 6.4 GB / 11 GB |
| **H3 draft** (8-step turbo), first frame + a line | pruned FL2VA NVFP4 11.7 GB, TE NVFP4, VAE int8 | 1344×768, 6 s (158 frames) | 390 s cold | 15.7 GB / 18.5 GB, GPU at 170 W (no thrashing) |
| **H3 draft continuing the take above** | same | 1344×768, 6 s | 445 s | same |
| Cut of the two, overlap trimmed | ffmpeg | 1920×1080, 12.25 s, −14 LUFS | 4 s | — |

For comparison, the same recipe's verified draft on a 32 GB RTX PRO 4500
with 62 GB of RAM took about 7.6 min per 5 s; NVFP4 plus Kitchen attention
puts the 16 GB card ahead of it.

- **The official 8-step turbo LoRA on the pruned base attaches 208 of its
  patches** ("208 patches attached"): its 51 AdaLN layers do not fit the
  pruned model, as FlowForgeLabAi measured. The take is still coherent.
- **Speech at 8 steps is approximate:** Whisper heard "Not today, Nagen."
  for "Not today. Not again." Voices are the weakest part of a turbo draft.
- **The continuation seam is invisible** in the cut: the six frames around
  the join show no jump in face, raincoat, light or street, and the second
  shot played its own action (she turns, the bus pulls in, she steps to it).
